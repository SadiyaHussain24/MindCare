Daily Habits

Five simple self-care tips

Small, consistent actions can make a real difference to how you feel.

Drink Water

Staying hydrated helps your body and brain function at their best, and can improve mood and focus.

Get Enough Sleep

Aim for 7–9 hours of rest each night. Good sleep supports emotional balance and clear thinking.

Exercise Regularly

Even a short daily walk can release endorphins and help reduce stress and anxiety naturally.

Talk to Someone You Trust

Sharing your feelings with a friend, family member, or counselor can lighten emotional weight.

Practice Deep Breathing

Slow, deep breaths activate your body's relaxation response and can calm anxious thoughts quickly.

Take Mindful Breaks

Step away from screens and stress for a few minutes — try our relaxing game.
